One funny text message your future self sends you — entry for the [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) bounty on make.fun.

## The message

> It's me, future you. Stop refreshing your ex's profile — in 10 years you marry the barista who spelled your name 'Greg.' Also floss. The dentist just charged me $4,200 for one tooth.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the message to stdout and writes it to `ENTRY_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`). Stdlib only, deterministic — two runs produce
byte-identical output.
